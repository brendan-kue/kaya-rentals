How Kaya Rentals works

Kaya Rentals is a free noticeboard for affordable homes in South Africa. Every listing rents for R6,000 a month or less and is posted by the property's owner.

For tenants

  1. Search by town, suburb, province, type and rent.
  2. Contact the owner directly by WhatsApp or phone. Searching is free and we never charge tenants.
  3. View the place, check the owner's ID matches the listing, and sign a written lease before you pay.

For owners

  1. Create a free owner account with your name, mobile number and email.
  2. Add your property: rent (maximum R6,000 a month), photos, area and what's included. Don't publish your street address.
  3. Confirm you own the property or have the owner's written permission to rent it out.
  4. We review every listing, usually within 24 hours. Owners who show us a municipal rates bill or title deed in their name plus ID get an Owner verified badge.
  5. Mark the listing as rented out when it's taken, and it disappears from search.

Agents and property companies may not list. You can have up to 5 listings.

Rental safety tips

  • Never pay a deposit or "holding fee" before viewing the property in person.
  • Ask to see the owner's ID and a rates bill in the same name.
  • Get a written lease. The Rental Housing Act requires your deposit to be kept in an interest-bearing account and an incoming inspection to be done together.
  • Be careful if the rent is far below the area's usual price, or the owner says they're overseas and can't meet.
  • Report suspicious listings with the "Report this listing" button. Rental disputes can go to your provincial Rental Housing Tribunal.

Privacy (POPIA)

We collect what we need to run the site: owners' name, email, mobile number and listing details and photos; and reports tenants submit. Listing details, the owner's first name and the contact number they choose are shown publicly so tenants can reach them. We use Google Firebase to store data and handle sign-in. We don't sell personal information.

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing rangariraimutasa@gmail.com, or delete your listings yourself from your dashboard. We delete closed accounts within 30 days, unless we must keep records for a legal reason or fraud investigation. You may complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Terms of use

  • You must be 18 or older to create an account.
  • Owners must own the property or hold the owner's written permission, and must list truthful details, real photos and the full monthly rent (R6,000 or less).
  • We may edit, reject or remove any listing and close any account, especially for fake listings, scams, discrimination or listings above R6,000.
  • Kaya Rentals is a noticeboard, not a rental agent. We are not a party to any lease, don't handle money, and don't guarantee any listing or person. Tenants and owners deal with each other at their own risk.
  • Listings must follow South African law, including the Rental Housing Act and the Constitution's protection against unfair discrimination.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of South Africa.
